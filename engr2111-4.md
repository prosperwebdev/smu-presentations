## This Weeks Assignment

<p class="mb-8 text-xl">Identify three friends, family members, teachers, or acquaintances who are willing to participate.</p>

::right::

### Submit the following:

- The names and relationship of the three people you selected.
- Your prediction of how each person would answer the questions below.
- Their actual responses to the questions below (written, video, phone call summary, etc.).
- A reflection on the differences between what you expected and what they said, including any surprises and what you plan to do with the information.

::left::

### Questions for participants:

- How would you describe my personality?
- What are my best attributes?
- What are my worst attributes?
- What do you wish I knew about myself that you believe I may not know yet?
- If there was one thing you wish I would work on related to my personality, what would that be?

## This Weeks Assignment

::left::

### Take at least 3 of these free EQ tests

- [Global Leadership Foundation: Free Emotional Intelligence Test](https://tests.globalleadershipfoundation.com/geit/eitest.html)
- [LEADx: Free EQ Test](https://leadx.org/eq/)
- [MTD Training: Free EQ Test](https://www.mtdtraining.com/free-eq-test)
- [Greater Good Science Center at UC Berkeley: Emotional Intelligence Quiz](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/quizzes/ei_quiz/take_quiz)
- [123test: Emotional Intelligence Test](https://www.123test.com/emotional-intelligence-test/123test)

::right::

### Required Tasks:

- Watch the "[Backward Brain Bicycle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MFzDaBzBlL0)" video and give a one paragraph description of you learning a new task
- Write a reflection of what you learned about yourself from each individual test and then compare the results of each test against each other?  What was interesting and/or surprising? What are your strengths and what are your opportunities for improvement? Discuss 2 areas you need to improve on and share actions you will take and practice to improve on those 2 areas
- Do something outside your comfort zone. Email your class manager about what you did and briefly describe the experience.
